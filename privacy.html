Privacy Policy – Purchase Tracker

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who uses this app

Purchase Tracker is used only by its owner, Mariusz Ogonowski, on his own Gmail account. No other users can sign in.

What data it accesses

The app requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete emails.

How the data is used

  • Emails are processed on the owner's own computer to decide whether they relate to a purchase.
  • Only emails identified as purchase-related are sent to an AI service to read out the vendor, date, amount and a short description.
  • The result is a list of purchases stored on the owner's computer for a personal household budget.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Google user data is used only to provide this feature to the owner, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Results are kept on the owner's computer and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
